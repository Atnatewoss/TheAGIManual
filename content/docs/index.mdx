# The AGI Manual

A comprehensive engineering handbook for understanding and building Artificial General Intelligence systems.

## What You'll Learn

This manual provides structured, practical knowledge for computer science students and AGI researchers. It covers foundational concepts, modern architectures, and cutting-edge frameworks like Hyperon, PRIMUS, and MeTTa.

## Philosophy

This manual emphasizes:

- **Practical Engineering**: Real implementations over theory alone
- **Clarity**: Technical precision without unnecessary jargon
- **Progression**: Logical learning paths from basics to advanced topics
- **Research-Driven**: Based on current AGI research and frameworks

Start with [Foundations](/docs/foundations/overview) if you're new to AGI, or jump directly to any section relevant to your work.
