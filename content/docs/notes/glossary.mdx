# Technical Glossary

### A
- **Atom**: The fundamental unit of data/code in the AtomSpace (Nodes and Links).
- **AtomSpace**: The distributed hypergraph knowledge store used in Hyperon.
- **Alignment**: The problem of ensuring AI goals match human values.

### G
- **GOFAI**: Good Old-Fashioned Artificial Intelligence (Symbolic AI).
- **Grounding**: Connecting abstract symbols to perceptual data or physical reality.

### H
- **Hypergraph**: A graph where an edge (link) can connect any number of vertices (nodes).
- **Hyperon**: The next-generation OpenCog AGI framework.

### M
- **MeTTa (Meta Type Talk)**: The primary programming language of Hyperon.
- **MOSES**: Meta-Optimizing Semantic Evolutionary Search (Program synthesis engine).

### N
- **Neuro-Symbolic**: AI that integrates neural networks and symbolic reasoning.

### P
- **PLN (Probabilistic Logic Networks)**: The uncertain reasoning engine in Hyperon.
- **PRIMUS**: Pattern Recognition and Integration Multi-Unit System (Perceptual architecture).

### T
- **Truth Value**: A metadata pair (Strength, Confidence) attached to an Atom.
