# Notes Overview

This section contains supplementary material to aid in your study of The AGI Manual. It includes reading lists, a technical glossary, and links to external research communities.

## Purpose
AGI is a vast field. Even a comprehensive manual cannot cover everything. These notes serve as a bridge to the broader world of AGI research.

## What's Inside

### 1. Research Resources
A curated list of books, papers, and courses for those who want to dive deeper into specific topics like Computational Neuroscience or Category Theory.

### 2. Technical Glossary
A quick reference for the dense terminology used throughout this manual (from "AtomSpace" to "Z-Calculus").

### 3. Community and Papers
Where to find the latest pre-prints and how to join the open-source development of Hyperon and MeTTa.
