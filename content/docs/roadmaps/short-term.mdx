# Short-Term Milestones (1-3 Years)

This roadmap focuses on the immediate engineering challenges required to build the foundation for more advanced AGI systems.

## 1. Hyperon V1.0 Release
- **Goal**: A stable, high-performance implementation of the AtomSpace and MeTTa.
- **Milestones**: Efficient distributed queries, strictly typed MeTTa core, and seamless Python integration.

## 2. Robust Neuro-Symbolic Connectors
- **Goal**: Standardized ways to map neural network activations to symbolic Atoms.
- **Milestones**: Differentiable pattern matching and automated "concept grounding" from visual data.

## 3. Advanced PLN Reasoner
- **Goal**: A reasoner capable of million-step uncertain inference.
- **Milestones**: Heuristic-guided search for reasoning paths and better consistency management in large knowledge bases.

## 4. Multi-Modal Benchmark Superiority
- **Goal**: Outperforming standard LLMs on tasks that require "common sense" reasoning and physical intuition.
- **Milestones**: Success on the ARC (Abstraction and Reasoning Corpus) and complex 3D simulation tasks.

## 5. Collaborative AGI Frameworks
- **Goal**: Facilitating researchers working on the same AtomSpace.
- **Milestones**: Version control for hypergraphs and decentralized knowledge sharing protocols.
